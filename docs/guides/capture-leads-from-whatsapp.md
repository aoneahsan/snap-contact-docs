# Capture leads from WhatsApp Web

**This guide shows how to turn unknown WhatsApp Web chats into SnapContact contacts using the browser extension — one at a time, or a whole group at once.** It captures only the chats you open, when you click. See [WhatsApp Web extension](../features/whatsapp-extension.md) for the feature reference.

## Before you start

- Install the SnapContact extension in Chrome or Edge (see [Installation](../getting-started/installation.md)).
- Sign in to the extension with your SnapContact account.
- Open [WhatsApp Web](https://web.whatsapp.com) and sign in there as usual.

## Save a single unknown number

1. Open the chat with the unknown contact.
2. Click the **Save to SnapContact** button the extension adds.
3. Optionally add a tag (for example `lead`) or a quick note.
4. Confirm. The contact is created in your account; if the number already exists, it's updated rather than duplicated.

## Bulk-save a group's members

1. Open the group chat and view its member list.
2. Use the extension's bulk-save action.
3. Optionally apply a tag to everyone you save.
4. Watch the progress indicator — you can cancel partway, and you'll get a summary of what saved and what didn't.

## Good practice

- Apply a tag at capture so the new leads are organized immediately.
- Capture is user-initiated and rate-limited by design; let a bulk save finish rather than hammering it.
- The extension reads only the chat you have open — it doesn't collect in the background.

## After capture

Your new contacts are normal SnapContact contacts. Add a [reminder](../features/reminders.md) to follow up, or open the contact later from any device — they sync to your account.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the extension capture chats in the background?** No. It acts only when you click, on the chat you're viewing.

**What happens if the number is already saved?** It's updated, not duplicated.

**Can I save everyone in a group?** Yes — bulk-save the members, with an optional tag, progress, and a summary.

**Which browsers work?** Chrome and Edge (Chromium). Other browsers aren't supported for the extension.
