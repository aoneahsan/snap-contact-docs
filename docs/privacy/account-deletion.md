# Account & data deletion

**You can export your SnapContact data at any time and permanently delete your account and all the data tied to it — your contacts, reminders, spaces, and uploaded files — from inside the app or by request.** SnapContact is built to honor data-portability and erasure rights.

## Export first (recommended)

Before deleting, take a copy: from the [Tools hub](../features/tools-import-export.md), export your contacts (for example to a spreadsheet) and back up your data. Once an account is deleted, its data is gone — an export is your safety net if you ever want to return or move elsewhere.

## Delete your account

Use the in-app account-deletion flow, or the canonical page at:

> [snapcontact.aoneahsan.com/account-deletion](https://snapcontact.aoneahsan.com/account-deletion)

Deleting your account removes your contact records and associated data from your Firestore profile and the files stored under your account via FilesHub. The app deletes the data it controls; for any third-party processor, deletion follows that provider's standard erasure path.

## What's removed

- Your contacts, tags, notes, lists, and timelines.
- Your reminders and spaces.
- Files you uploaded (card images, space attachments).
- Your account identity.

Local device preferences (like your chosen theme) are device-side; uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes those.

## Need help?

If you can't complete deletion yourself, email **aoneahsan@gmail.com** from the address on your account and request erasure. See also the in-app [privacy policy](https://snapcontact.aoneahsan.com/privacy).

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I delete my account myself?** Yes — use the in-app flow or the [account-deletion page](https://snapcontact.aoneahsan.com/account-deletion).

**Can I get my data before deleting?** Yes — export and back up from the Tools hub first.

**What gets deleted?** Your contacts, reminders, spaces, uploaded files, and account identity.

**Is deletion reversible?** No — that's why exporting first is recommended.

**Who do I contact for help?** aoneahsan@gmail.com from your account email.
