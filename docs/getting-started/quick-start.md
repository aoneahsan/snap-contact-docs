# Quick Start

**This guide takes you from a fresh sign-in to a saved contact with a follow-up reminder in about five minutes.** It uses manual entry so it works everywhere; once you've done it once, the scanner and import paths will feel obvious. Have the [web app](https://snapcontact.aoneahsan.com) or the Android app open and be signed in (see [Account & sign-in](./account-and-sign-in.md)).

## 1. Create a contact

1. Open **Contacts** from the main navigation.
2. Tap **Add contact**.
3. Enter a name and at least one phone or email. Phone numbers are validated and formatted as you type.
4. Optionally add a company, title, and city.
5. Save.

You now have a contact. The fields you skipped stay empty and editable — nothing is required beyond a name and one way to reach the person.

## 2. Add context: a tag and a note

Open the contact you just created.

1. Add a **tag** — for example `lead` or `event-2026`. Type to create a new tag or pick an existing one.
2. Write a short **note** — where you met, what they asked for, the next step.

Tags drive segments and filtering; notes are searchable, so a detail you jot down today is findable later even if you forget the person's name.

## 3. Set a follow-up reminder

Still on the contact:

1. Choose **Add reminder**.
2. Pick a quick template (**Follow up**, **Send quote**, or **Book call**) or write your own title.
3. Set a due date and time, and a priority.
4. Save.

On Android, you'll get a notification when it's due (after you allow notifications the first time). On the web, the reminder appears in your **Today / Upcoming / Overdue** views.

## 4. Find it again with search

Go back to **Contacts** and type part of the name, company, tag, or even a word from your note. SnapContact uses fuzzy matching, so you don't need the exact spelling — a close guess finds the right person.

## 5. (Optional) Try a faster capture

Now that you understand the shape of a contact, try a quicker path:

- **Scan a business card** — see [Scan a business card](../guides/scan-a-business-card.md). The camera reads the card on-device and pre-fills the fields for you to confirm.
- **Capture from WhatsApp Web** — see [Capture leads from WhatsApp Web](../guides/capture-leads-from-whatsapp.md) if you've installed the extension.
- **Import in bulk** — see [Import your existing contacts](../guides/import-existing-contacts.md) to bring in a whole list from Google, Excel/CSV, or vCard.

## What you've learned

You created a contact, attached a tag and a note, set a reminder that surfaces in the Today view, and found the contact again by fuzzy search. That loop — capture, add context, schedule the next action — is the whole point of SnapContact.

## Frequently asked questions

**What's the minimum I need to save a contact?** A name and one phone or email. Everything else is optional.

**Will I get notified about the reminder?** On Android, yes, once you allow notifications. On the web, due items appear in your Today/Upcoming/Overdue views.

**Do I have to type contacts in?** No — manual entry is just the most universal path. Scanning, WhatsApp capture, and import are all faster for the right situation.

**Can I edit a contact later?** Yes. Open it and edit any field, tag, or note; the per-contact timeline records the changes.
