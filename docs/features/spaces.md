# Spaces

**A space is a lightweight area you create for a single client or project, where you can post updates, attach files, and pin the contacts involved — so everything about that engagement lives in one place tied to the people in it.** Spaces are the collaboration layer of SnapContact, intentionally simpler than a full project tool: posts, files, and pinned contacts, not Gantt charts.

## What a space contains

| Element | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Posts / messages | Share updates and keep a running log for the client or project. |
| File attachments | Keep relevant documents with the work they belong to. |
| Pinned contacts | Surface the people who matter in this space for quick reference. |

A space can be personal (just you) or shared. Basic roles distinguish the owner from members so it's clear who created the space.

## Why use a space instead of tags

Tags organize your whole contact list horizontally; a space organizes one engagement vertically. When a client involves several contacts, a few documents, and an ongoing thread of updates, a space keeps that bundle together instead of scattering it across individual contact records. You still reach the contacts through your normal list — pinning them in a space is an additional view, not a separate copy.

## Files in spaces

Attachments uploaded to a space are stored under your account via the FilesHub file-storage API, with upload progress shown for larger files. This keeps file handling free of a paid storage backend while still tying each document to the space it belongs to.

## Scope and honesty

Spaces in version 1.1 cover posts, file attachments, and pinned contacts with owner/member roles. Richer collaboration — granular roles, phone-based invites, and mentions — is roadmap, not shipped. The docs describe only what exists today.

## Frequently asked questions

**What's the difference between a space and a tag?** A tag labels contacts across your whole list. A space bundles the posts, files, and pinned contacts for one client or project in a single place.

**Can I attach files to a space?** Yes — files are stored under your account via FilesHub, with progress shown for larger uploads.

**Are spaces shared with a team?** They can be. A space can be personal or shared, with owner and member roles.

**Do pinned contacts get duplicated into the space?** No. Pinning is a reference to the existing contact, not a copy.
