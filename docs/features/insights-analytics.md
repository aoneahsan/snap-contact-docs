# Insights & analytics

**Insights is a set of charts and reports computed over your own contact list — growth over time, segment breakdowns, tag distribution, where contacts came from, and how your network connects — that show real numbers from your data, not placeholder trends.** When you have no data yet, the charts say so with an honest empty state rather than drawing a fake line.

## What you can see

| View | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Growth | How your contact count grows by week, month, and year. |
| Segments | Breakdown of your contacts by tag-based segments. |
| Tag analytics | Which tags you actually use across your contacts. |
| Source attribution | Where your contacts came from (scanner, WhatsApp, import, manual). |
| Network | A map and metrics of how your contacts relate, plus an activity heatmap. |
| Reports | A builder to assemble the figures you care about. |

These live on the in-app **Insights** surface and are reached from the dashboard.

## Honest empty states

A recurring design choice: SnapContact does not invent placeholder data to make charts look full. The growth chart stays empty until you add contacts; the tag chart populates only once you start tagging; an engagement score over a contact with no history is honestly low rather than a flattering default. This keeps the numbers trustworthy — what you see is your data.

## Per-contact insights

Beyond the list-wide views, individual contacts carry their own signals on the contact-detail screen: engagement scoring, a quality score, and similar metrics derived from the contact's real timeline activity. An empty history produces a low, honest score, not a fabricated one.

## Privacy of analytics

Insights are computed over the contacts in your account. They are separate from the product-usage analytics SnapContact collects to improve the app (which are covered in [Data & privacy](../privacy/data-and-privacy.md)). The charts you see in Insights are about your contacts, for you.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is the data in Insights real or sample data?** Real — it's computed from your own contacts. Empty states are shown when there's nothing to chart, instead of placeholder trends.

**What can I measure?** Growth over time, segment and tag breakdowns, source attribution, network relationships, and an activity heatmap, plus a reports builder.

**Are there per-contact metrics?** Yes — engagement and quality scores on the contact-detail screen, derived from that contact's timeline.

**Do Insights share my data?** No — they're computed over your account's contacts and shown only to you.
