# Reminders

**A reminder is a dated follow-up attached to a contact, with a title, a due time, and a priority, that SnapContact surfaces in Today / Upcoming / Overdue views and notifies you about on Android.** Reminders are how the app enforces follow-up discipline: the lead you captured is only valuable if you act before it goes cold.

## Anatomy of a reminder

| Field | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Title | What to do — write your own or pick a template. |
| Linked contact | Who it's about. |
| Due date & time | When it should surface and notify. |
| Priority | How urgent, so high-priority items stand out. |

## Quick templates

Three built-in templates cover the most common follow-ups so you don't retype them:

- **Follow up** — a general nudge to re-engage.
- **Send quote** — for a pending proposal.
- **Book call** — to schedule a conversation.

You can always write a custom title instead; the templates are shortcuts, not a fixed list.

## Today, Upcoming, Overdue

Reminders are organized into three views so you always know what needs attention:

- **Today** — due now; your daily action list.
- **Upcoming** — scheduled ahead, so nothing sneaks up.
- **Overdue** — past due and not yet done, so slipped follow-ups stay visible instead of disappearing.

You can mark a reminder done, snooze it, or reschedule from these views.

## Notifications

On Android, a due reminder fires a notification (and updates the app badge), provided you've allowed notifications — the permission is requested when you first set something up, not at launch. The app also supports birthday reminders. On the web, due items live in the Today/Upcoming/Overdue views rather than as OS notifications. Reminders created offline are saved locally and reconciled when you reconnect.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do reminders notify me?** On Android, yes, once you allow notifications. On the web, due reminders appear in the Today/Upcoming/Overdue views.

**Can I snooze or reschedule?** Yes — mark done, snooze, or reschedule from any of the three views.

**Are there templates?** Yes: Follow up, Send quote, and Book call, plus your own custom titles.

**Do reminders need an internet connection?** No — you can create them offline; they sync when you reconnect.

**Is there a birthday reminder?** Yes, the app can remind you about contact birthdays alongside your follow-ups.
